Pricing FAQ

Is there a free trial for the Team plan?

Yes — 14 days, no credit card required, with every Team feature unlocked. You can invite your whole team during the trial.

What counts as an agent run?

One completed pass through the Observe → Plan → Act → Verify loop for a single task, regardless of how many tool calls it makes internally.

Can I change plans later?

Anytime. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. Nothing is locked in.

What happens if I go over my included agent runs?

We'll notify you before you hit the limit. Additional runs are billed at a flat per-run rate — no surprise overage fees.

Do you offer discounts for startups or nonprofits?

We do. Reach out through the contact form with a bit about your organization and we'll take a look.
